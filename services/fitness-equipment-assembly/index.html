Questions about fitness equipment assembly

What fitness equipment can you assemble?

Smith machines, power racks, weight benches, treadmills, ellipticals, exercise bikes, steppers and other home gym equipment, plus e-bikes.

Do you bring your own tools?

Yes. We bring our own tools, follow the manufacturer's instructions, check that everything is stable and level, and clean up when we finish.

What should I have ready before you arrive?

Please have the boxes in the room where the equipment will be used, with the manual and all parts. Send us a photo or the model name when you contact us so we can confirm the job.
