04 / FAQ

Common questions

How much does furniture assembly cost in Redondo Beach?

Furniture assembly for homeowners is $65 per hour with one worker or $110 per hour with two workers, with a 2-hour minimum. Commercial projects are priced by agreement.

How much does TV mounting cost?

TV mounting is $85 for TVs up to 55 inches, $100 for 55 to 65 inch TVs, and from $120 for TVs over 65 inches.

How much does interior painting cost?

Painting starts from $300 per room. That price does not include wall patching or ceiling painting, which are quoted based on the job.

How much does wallpaper hanging cost?

Wallpaper hanging starts from $85 per hour with one worker or $150 per hour with two workers.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Redondo Beach, CA and serve the South Bay within about 15 miles, including Hermosa Beach, Manhattan Beach, Torrance, El Segundo, Palos Verdes, Lawndale and Gardena.
